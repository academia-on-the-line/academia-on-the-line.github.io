Podcast abstract
OpenAI's recent disproof of Erdös' unit distance conjecture surprised much of the mathematics community and prompted many researchers to reconsider the capabilities of modern reasoning models. In our inaugural episode, Melanie Matchett Wood and Daniel Litt join us to discuss the mathematics behind the result, the nine author companion paper written by leading mathematicians, and the broader implications of AI for mathematical research and graduate education.

About our Guest

Daniel Litt is an assistant professor of mathematics at the University of Toronto, working at the intersection of algebraic geometry, number theory, and topology. After receiving his PhD in 2015 from Stanford, he held positions at the University of Georgia, the Institute for Advanced Study, and Columbia University. He is a Sloan Research Fellow, a Fellow of the AMS, a recipient of the Ontario Early Researcher Award, and a recipient of the 2025 Cathleen Synge Morawetz Prize. In the fall, he'll be the Benedict H. Gross distinguished visitor at Harvard University.
